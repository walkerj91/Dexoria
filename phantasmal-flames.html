Release Date: November 2025
Series: Mega Evolution
Cards: 130

Phantasmal Flames is a haunting expansion that blends eerie Ghost-types with blazing Fire-type power. Featuring mysterious Pokémon like Gengar, Chandelure, and Skeledirge, this set focuses on trickery, status effects, and lingering damage mechanics. With dark, atmospheric artwork and highly sought-after alternate art cards, Phantasmal Flames stands out for its sinister aesthetic and collector appeal.

Top Chase Cards

Mega Gengar ex (Hyper Rare)

A stunning secret rare featuring a ghostly, gold-etched Mega Gengar against a glittering textured backdrop. A must-have for Ghost-type collectors.

Mega Charizard X ex (Special Illustration Rare)

The undisputed crown jewel of the set. Features breathtaking, story-driven artwork tracking the evolution of Charmander up to its fierce, blue-flamed Mega form.

Mega Charizard X ex (Mega Hyper Rare)

A premium, maximum-rarity gold card boasting intricate texturing and massive visual impact. Features roaring artwork by Takuyoa that commands top market value.

Mega Diancie ex (Special Illustration Rare)

Highly praised for its glittering, jewel-toned holographic patterns. This gorgeous alternate-art card makes full use of the unique Mega Evolution frame layout.
