Release Date: September 2025
Series: Mega Evolution
Cards: 188

Mega Evolution is a highly anticipated set focused on the return of powerful Mega-Evolved Pokémon. Featuring fan-favourites like Mega Charizard, Mega Lucario, and Mega Gardevoir, this set brings explosive attacks, enhanced abilities, and dynamic gameplay mechanics. With stunning artwork, alternate art cards, and rare full-art Megas, this expansion is a must-have for both competitive players and collectors alike.

Top Chase Cards

Mega Lucario ex (Mega Hyper Rare)

The ultimate grail of the expansion. Features a fierce Mega Lucario radiating with golden aura highlights, enhanced by intricate, deep-grooved texturing that commands maximum market premium.

Mega Gardevoir ex (Mega Hyper Rare)

A breath-taking secret rare boasting an elegant, gold-etched layout. Its dazzling, textured golden backdrop makes it an absolute masterpiece for high-end character collectors.

Mega Lucario ex (Special Illustration Rare)

A stunning, action-packed narrative piece that captures Mega Lucario mid-battle. The striking color palette and dynamic framing make it a top flagship target of the block.

Mega Venusaur ex (Special Illustration Rare)

Pure Kanto nostalgia brought to life with vibrant, sprawling flora artwork. This massive Gen 1 favorite features highly detailed special illustration framing that appeals to all generations of collectors.
