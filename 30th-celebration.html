Release Date: September 2026
Series: Scarlet & Violet
Cards: 199

The 30th Celebration set is a massive commemorative special expansion to mark the 30th anniversary of the Pokémon Trading Card Game. This highly anticipated mega-set blends nostalgic historical callbacks with futuristic card designs, ensuring that every single card in the expansion is a holofoil. Some of the most iconic Pokémon from the past three decades are featured in this set, including the new Mew ex (Futuristic Rare), Mewtwo ex (Futuristic Rare), fan favourite Jirachi ex (Special Illustration Rare) and Pikachu ex (Special Illustration Rare).
