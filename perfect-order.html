The pulse of the city beats in sync with the Pokémon and people who call it home—and preserving the order of it all is Mega Zygarde ex, the Legendary Pokémon who embodies strength in numbers! With threatening forces held at bay, peaceful days are ahead for the city’s residents, including Pokémon like Mega Clefable ex, Mega Starmie ex, and Meowth ex, in the Pokémon TCG: Mega Evolution—Perfect Order expansion!

Top Chase Cards

Meowth ex (Special Illustration Rare)

The undisputed top grail of the set. Features highly stylized, playful artwork capturing Meowth ex roaming the city streets, complete with glittering special rare texture work.

Mega Zygarde ex (Mega Hyper Rare)

A premium, maximum-rarity gold card that stands as the ultimate defender of Lumiose City. Its deep-grooved golden texturing and massive visual presence command a huge secondary market premium.

Mega Zygarde ex (Special Illustration Rare)

A breathtaking alternate-art narrative piece showcasing the Legendary Complete Forme Zygarde. Its striking, complex background composition makes it a premium target for high-end collectors.

Mega Clefable ex (Special Illustration Rare)

A whimsical and vibrant alternate-art masterpiece celebrating Clefable's newly debuted Mega form. Its soft, enchanting color palette and heavy texturing anchor the set's secret rare tier.
