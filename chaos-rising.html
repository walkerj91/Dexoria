Release Date: May 2026
Series: Mega Evolution
Cards: 122

Mega Evolution is a highly anticipated set focused on the return of powerful Mega-Evolved Pokémon. Featuring fan-favourites like Mega Charizard, Mega Lucario, and Mega Gardevoir, this set brings explosive attacks, enhanced abilities, and dynamic gameplay mechanics. With stunning artwork, alternate art cards, and rare full-art Megas, this expansion is a must-have for both competitive players and collectors alike.

Top Chase Cards

Mega Greninja ex (Mega Hyper Rare)

A maximum-rarity gold secret rare showcasing a fierce, gold-etched Mega Greninja silhouette. Its brilliant golden texturing makes it a premium, top-tier pull for high-end master set collectors.

Mega Greninja ex (Special Illustration Rare)

The undisputed crown jewel of the set. Features spectacular, action-packed alternate artwork of Mega Greninja mid-stride, boasting heavy surface texturing that has sent its market value skyrocketing.

Cinccino ex (Special Illustration Rare)

A highly sought-after, whimsical alternate-art card that has become an overnight fan favourite. Features gorgeous storytelling framing that commands a strong premium on the secondary market.

Mega Floette ex (Special Illustration Rare)

Captures the catalyst of the entire expansion's story arc in breathtaking detail. This beautifully textured secret rare features Floette unleashing beautiful but chaotic urban energy.
